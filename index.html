About the group

Build, share, and learn in the open

Chicago Open Bioinformatics brings together makers, scientists, developers, and curious newcomers to build reusable tools for community biology. This GitHub Pages site highlights active project spaces and makes it easy to discover what the group is working on.

We are proud to be part of the broader ChiTownBio community and to champion the open-science mission of the Open Bioinformatics Foundation. Explore both organizations to find events, collaborators, and open bioinformatics work happening beyond this site.
